Startups, companies & junior jobs by niche and region

Niche

YC data comes from yc-oss/api, an unofficial, community-maintained copy of Y Combinator’s public company directory. Not affiliated with Y Combinator. “Hiring” and “remote” labels come from YC’s listing. Check the company’s YC profile to confirm them.

A hand-picked list of cloud, cybersecurity and automation companies, including many European startups that aren’t YC-backed, with links to each company’s official website and careers page. For companies marked “Hiring”, open roles are counted live from their public careers board (Ashby or Greenhouse). The “YC-backed” label appears when a company’s website also appears in the YC data. It doesn’t cover every company. Always check the careers page.

Jobs are matched by title, and level labels are a best guess. Always read the original listing to confirm location, remote rules and experience required. Listings belong to their sources, and every job links to its original page.

Saved companies stay in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.

Data sources: yc-oss/api (YC companies, unofficial) · Jobs from Remotive, Remote OK, Jobicy, Himalayas and companies’ own careers pages (Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever) · Company directory from official company websites.

Startup Radar doesn’t cover every startup or vacancy in the world. It’s an independent project, not affiliated with any of these sources.
